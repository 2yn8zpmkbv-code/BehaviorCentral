Good to know

  • Instructors can't change the day or time of a scheduled final.
  • If your class start time doesn't match a time above, your instructor picks the closest exam slot. Check your syllabus.
  • Missed a final? Notify your instructor within 24 hours, or you'll automatically receive a failing grade for the exam. Absences are excused only for unavoidable, documented circumstances (e.g., a medical or family emergency).
  • Three or more exams in one day? Tell the instructor of your latest exam at least one week before finals; they'll reschedule it.

Note to Researchers

The IRB only accepts submissions directly from faculty. Students may not be Principal Investigator (PI). A faculty advisor/sponsor must always be listed as PI and submit the application. No exceptions!

Internships

Real-world experience for credit, through Flagler

Internships solve the classic dilemma: "Jobs require experience, but how do I get experience without a job?" You gain real-world practice, build your resume, and strengthen graduate applications.

Requirements

Credits1–3 credit hours
Eligibility3.0 GPA and junior/senior standing
TimelineMost internships take 2–3 months to set up; some sites have fixed application windows.
Start earlyIdeally begin before pre-registration advising in the semester before your internship.

Why Do an Internship?

  • Clarify interests and goals: test-drive career paths before investing time and money after graduation
  • Develop professional skills: clinical, research, communication, and office skills that boost job readiness
  • Build your network: supervisors and staff can become mentors and references
  • Strengthen grad-school candidacy: supervisors can write strong recommendation letters and help with goal statements (Landrum & Clark, 2005)
  • Apply classroom learning: turn theories and methods into real-world practice
  • Know your strengths: learn what you do well and what to develop next

3

Set up an appointment with your advisor

Advising opens Tuesday, September 29. Your advisor will reach out (contact them if you haven't heard from them) and follow their instructions to schedule a meeting. Find your advisor under Faculty & Staff. Some advisors may ask you to complete the pre-advising quiz first.

A suggested script for advisors and students.

1

Review the Advising Worksheet together

Students should review their worksheet before the meeting and bring a list of errors or concerns. Together, confirm that:

  • All courses display correctly under the major, minor, and CORE requirements
  • Completed courses are counted properly and in the right place
  • Transfer credits or substitutions are reflected accurately

If something is wrong, decide on the correction (e.g., course substitution or worksheet correction; see Forms & Contacts) and next steps.

2

Check graduation progress & milestones

Confirm credits earned and remaining, catalog year, and any GPA/residency minimums. Then check time-sensitive milestones:

  • FlagSHIP: ideally completed by your second year; confirm it's planned or done and shows on the worksheet
  • Co-curriculars: check your count and plan the rest; record them in Saints Connect (instructions)
  • Application for Graduation: you're eligible at 60 credit hours, and it's due no later than the semester before you graduate (deadlines under Registration)
FlagSHIP Canvas ↗ Saints Connect ↗ Application to Graduate ↗
3

Program-specific courses & requirements

Review courses that could delay graduation if missed: fall- or spring-only courses, prerequisite chains, and capstone or methods courses.

5

Clear for registration & confirm your window

The advisor applies registration clearance. Then both of you verify nothing will block registration:

  • Holds: check MyFlagler for holds that block registration (AR, FS, GR; see Registration) and resolve them
  • Class standing: confirm FR/SO/JR/SR is correct; fix it right away if it's too low
  • Registration time: confirm your date and time (CACE emails it on Monday, September 28). Questions? cace@flagler.edu. Withdrawing from a course before registration can lower your credits and push back your date without notice.
  • Part-time or withdrawing? Talk to Financial Aid first: federal loans may be reduced (see Registration)
6

Advising beyond class selection

Use the meeting to talk about bigger goals:

  • Community: clubs, organizations, and service. See Community
  • Development: careers, internships, grad school, and leadership. See Internships
  • Research: independent and faculty-led projects and conferences. See Research
  • Support: campus resources. See Support

How to Register

  1. Accept the Registration Agreement. Log in to MyFlagler → Add/Drop Courses, set the term to 2026–2027 Spring Semester St. Augustine Day, and accept the agreement.
  2. Add courses when your window opens. Log out and back in, open Add/Drop Courses, set the term, search for the courses on your plan, check the box, and click Add Courses. Repeat until your schedule is complete.
  3. Waitlist full classes. Add the class, then join the waitlist. If a seat opens you'll get a Flagler email and have 24 hours to respond. Keep a backup course so you stay full-time, and drop it if your waitlisted class opens.

Part-Time Enrollment & Federal Loans

Starting July 1, 2026, if you have Federal Direct Loans and enroll in, drop, or withdraw to 6–11 credit hours, your loans will be prorated. With 1–5 credit hours, you're not eligible for federal loans. Before enrolling part-time, dropping to part-time, or withdrawing from a class, talk to Financial Aid about how it affects this term and next.
